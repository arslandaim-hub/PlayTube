
**What's changed:**
### Playback & Audio
* **Audio-Only Mode:** Stream or play media in the background without loading video.
* **Playback Controls:** Added Volume Boost, an A-B Repeat loop, and an interactive Video Chapters sheet.
* **Bug Fixes:** Resolved issues with audio track selection and playback mode switching.

### Downloads & Subtitles
* **Faster, Reliable Downloads:** Implemented parallel chunked downloading for improved download resilience.
* **Offline Subtitles:** Added support for downloading subtitles for offline viewing, complete with WebVTT sanitization.

### Home Screen, Settings & UI
* **PlayTube Engine Insights:** Added a dedicated section in Settings to view user statistical data learned by the PlayTube engine.
* **Category Chips:** Quickly filter the home feed by Trending, Music, Gaming, and more.
* **Content Blocking:** Block specific videos and channels directly from the home screen.
* **Visual Redesign:** Introduced a cleaner, simplified bottom navigation bar and multiple new theme color options.

### Localization & General
* Added support for the Russian language as well.
* Various under-the-hood performance improvements and minor bug fixes.
