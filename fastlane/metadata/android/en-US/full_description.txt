PlayTube is a fast, private, and feature-rich YouTube client for Android.

Key Features:
- Fluid Glass UI.
- Background Play.
- Picture-in-Picture (PIP).
- Comments.
- Subtitles.
- Video Downloads.
- Volume/brightness & seek gestures.
- Push up & down to enable/disable fullscreen mode.
- Subscription Management.

PlayTube eliminates software bloat to ensure a lightweight, efficient video playback experience with modern app design.
